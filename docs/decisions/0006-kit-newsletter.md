# 0006. Kit's free plan for the newsletter

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-10-05
- **Issues:** —

## Decision

Use Kit (formerly ConvertKit) on its free Newsletter plan, which covered up to 10,000
subscribers in 2026. Embed its sign-up form on the site.

## Alternatives considered

- **Buttondown:** nicer for Markdown devlogs, but free only to 100 subscribers, then paid.

## Consequences

- The form posts to Kit; the site stays static.
