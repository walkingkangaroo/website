# 0021. The about section has no photo

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-10-07
- **Issues:** KAN-759
- **Round:** none: Grant's note on KAN-759

## Context

The B2 board and the content round planned Grant's photo as a 160 px circle beside the bio (brief
step 3). Grant's note on KAN-759, at build time, said he doesn't want a photo in the bio.

## Decision

No photo. The about section is the heading and the bio only. The photo slot is removed from the
component and the site data.

## Alternatives considered

- **Keep the slot empty for later:** Grant wants it out of the design.

## Consequences

- Nothing to supply.
- The only images on the site are now the project covers.
